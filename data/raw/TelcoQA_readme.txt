Dataset Construction (TelcoQA): We curated a specialized dataset from Enterprise Community Forum exchanges focusing on Broadband and Landline diagnostics. Natural Language Question-Ground Truth (NLQ-GT) pairs were synthesised by designating the initial user inquiry as the Question and the verified community solution as the Ground Truth. We recognise the one-to-many mapping inherent in telecommunications troubleshooting; for instance, a single symptom like "latent connectivity" may stem from local hardware, residential configuration, or provider-side service disruptions, necessitating a nuanced evaluation of model-generated solutions.
